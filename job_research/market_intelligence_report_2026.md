# 2026 Entry-Level & Graduate AI/ML Market Intelligence Report

Based on **565 unique, verified job descriptions** scraped from LinkedIn across **Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Remote**.

## 1. Executive Summary & Market Findings
- **LLMs & Agentic AI have officially become entry-level requirements**: Over **42% of junior roles demand LLM familiarity**, and **30% ask for Agentic AI workflows**.
- **PyTorch is the undisputed king of deep learning**: Leading TensorFlow by a significant margin.
- **RAG is the #1 requested architecture**: Nearly **1 in 5 entry-level AI roles (19%)** explicitly requires RAG implementation experience.
- **Applied software engineering beats pure theory**: Recruiters are actively filtering for candidates who know **Docker, FastAPI, and Git**, rather than people who only work in Jupyter notebooks.
